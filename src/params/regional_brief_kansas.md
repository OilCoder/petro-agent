# Regional brief — Ness County, Kansas (Schaben area)

Reference DATA for the analyst. Facts and citations only: nothing here directs an
action, a method, or an interval — reading the rocks stays your job.

## Geological setting

- The producing interval in this area is Mississippian (Osagian–Meramecian) carbonate,
  developed on the Central Kansas Uplift flank. Source: Kansas Geological Survey (KGS)
  publications on the Schaben field demonstration project (Ness County). Exact member
  tops per lease: unverified here — por confirmar against KGS well records.
- Reservoir lithology in this field was measured in this project as dolomitic: the
  M-N and Umaa cross-plot analysis of the field study (summit report, outputs of this
  repository) clusters in the dolomite region, with chert influence. Source: this
  project's own engine-computed lithology cross-plots — a project measurement, not an
  external assumption.
- Regionally, the Mississippian section sits below Pennsylvanian shales; shallow
  Cretaceous/Permian sections above are not the objective in these leases (KGS,
  general stratigraphy of west-central Kansas; lease-level confirmation pending).

## Log-era caveats (vintage acquisitions)

- Wells logged before the mid-1970s in this region commonly carry a count-rate
  neutron record (CPS/API units, single detector) instead of a compensated porosity
  reading; such records need a transform before any porosity reading, and that
  transform degrades precision. Fact of the acquisition era (SPWLA literature on
  legacy neutron logs; general).
- Natural gamma in carbonates can respond to uranium associated with fractures or
  stylolites rather than clay; a hot gamma streak in a carbonate is not automatically
  shale (SPWLA/textbook fact, general).
- Old paper-digitized records may carry depth-shift and unit inconsistencies; header
  units deserve skepticism (era fact, general).

## Water and pressure context

- Mississippian brines in west-central Kansas are saline; produced-water salinity
  varies by lease and reported values in headers are frequently absent or nominal.
  Lease-level Rw: unverified here — por confirmar against water catalogs.
