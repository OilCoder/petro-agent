Conclusions

Across the four wells evaluated, the two contributing wells return a cross-well net pay P50 averaging 11.2 m (ranging 5.6–18.7 m, not a summed figure), but the low mean net-to-gross of 0.037 points to thin, poorly developed reservoir intervals that materially limit standalone potential. With half the wells abstaining, confidence at the field scale remains low and the reported range should be treated as indicative rather than representative. The highest-leverage action is to resolve the two abstentions—through log reprocessing or reevaluation of cutoffs—to firm up the net pay distribution and confirm whether the modest NTG reflects a genuine reservoir limitation or a data-quality gap.
